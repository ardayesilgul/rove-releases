## Why Rove?

Our computers hold thousands of scattered photos, contracts, PDFs, and notes accumulated over the years. Yet finding a specific memory or an important receipt often takes minutes, and cloud-based AI tools ask you to upload all your personal photos and documents to their servers.

**Rove changes this.** Built from the ground up for Windows, Rove runs deep AI algorithms entirely on your own local hardware. It is extremely fast and lightweight, works quietly in the background, and never leaks your data to the internet.

This lets you browse your photos together, group people, and find all your documents effortlessly without uploading anything to cloud services. From the **Photos** tab, you can view the photos on your computer just like a phone gallery, neatly organized day by day from newest to oldest.

## Key Features

### 1. Smart & 100% Private Face & Photo Albums

Rove scans the faces in your selected photo folders, brings together photos that appear to belong to the same person, and presents them for you to name. Once you assign a name to someone, you can view all their photos across your library together.

The face recognition system may occasionally make mistakes. Confirming suggested matches and correcting wrong ones helps Rove learn and recognize people in your collection much more accurately.

* **Learns Over Time:** Rove doesn't rely on simple static templates. As new photos arrive across different years, lighting conditions, beards, or glasses, it dynamically updates each person's identity profile and learns to recognize them more accurately every day.
* **Automatic Grouping:** Gathers everyone into their own album without requiring manual tagging.
* **Zero Cloud Uploads:** All face detection and biometric processing runs directly on your computer's CPU and GPU. Your memories never leave your disk.
* **One-Click Corrections:** When you remove a false match (objects, clothing prints, cartoon characters, trinkets, etc.) from an album, the AI immediately clears that mistake from its memory and won't present it again. If someone else's photo was assigned to the wrong album, you can simply reassign it to the right person, reducing the chance of the model repeating that mistake.

### 2. Deep In-Document Text Search

Search directly through the body text of contracts, invoices, homework notes, or spreadsheets even when the keyword does not appear in the file name.

<br/>

<div align="center">

<br/>

* **Content-Aware Indexing:** Queries like `"Termination and Indemnity"` locate files named simply `Service_Agreement_2024.docx` in milliseconds.
* **Contextual Text Highlighting:** Highlights the exact matching clause or sentence directly in yellow inside the search result card.
* **Wide Format Support:** Supports native full-text search across `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx`, `.txt`, and `.udf` formats.

### 3. OCR Text Detection in Scans & Images

Find invoices, deeds, receipts, vehicle registrations, or screenshots by searching for amounts and words visible directly inside the image.

<br/>

* **Zero Pre-Tagging Needed:** Instantly bring up an invoice by searching `"Total $4,850"` even if the file is just named `Scan_Invoice_0921.jpg`.
* **Side-by-Side Inspection:** Review the original scanned image alongside the recognized OCR text block with highlighted matches.
* **Quick Actions:** Copy the recognized text to your clipboard with a single click, open the image, or reveal its location in Windows Explorer.

### 5. Live Dynamic Island at the Top of Your Screen

The Dynamic Island provides media controls and a temporary holding area for your files in a subtle capsule at the top of your screen. While you continue working, you can drop a file onto the island to keep it close at hand. Open **Convert** under file actions to choose from available formats; Rove creates a converted copy for you. Supported formats depend on the file type.

<br/>

<div align="center">
<img src="assets/dynamic_island.gif" alt="Rove Dynamic Island Showcase" width="80%" />
</div>

<br/>

The Dynamic Island morphs into different tools depending on what you're doing. Control media playback right from the island; skip tracks and adjust volume. Choose between three distinct views.

When you drop a file onto the island, relevant actions open up based on the file type. For images, you get **convert**, **compress**, and **extract image text**; for documents, you get **convert**, **extract text**, and **open file**. Conversion options vary by file type. Actions preserve the original file and create a new copy alongside it.

In the Functional view, text snippets and file paths you copy to the clipboard collect in a temporary shelf, where you can re-copy any item back to your clipboard. **Quick Note**, accessible from the same area, saves your short notes so you can pick up right where you left off.

When browser tabs approach the top of the screen, the island tucks 5px upward so it never blocks you from closing tabs; it hides completely in full-screen games or video playback.

---

### 6. Built for Pure Speed, Not Bloat

Rove runs on an optimized, native local architecture. Photo and file scans run smoothly in the background, allowing you to navigate other parts of the application and continue your computer work uninterrupted.
